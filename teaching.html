TEACHING PHILOSOPHY

Education Beyond the Classroom

As a lecturer and entrepreneurship educator, Titus Nyuma Korfeh is committed to helping students develop both theoretical understanding and practical perspectives.

His approach emphasizes critical thinking, entrepreneurship, problem-solving, business awareness, and the ability to connect academic concepts with real-world situations.

He believes that effective education should encourage curiosity, collaboration, creativity, and continuous learning.
