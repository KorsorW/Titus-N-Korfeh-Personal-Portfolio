PROFESSIONAL PROFILE

A Journey Built on Learning & Impact

Titus Nyuma Korfeh is an educator, researcher, entrepreneurship professional, and academic with professional experience spanning education, accounting, audit, taxation, compliance, risk management, business development, and entrepreneurship.

He currently serves as a full-time Lecturer and Minor Program Coordinator in Entrepreneurship at the University of Liberia, where he contributes to entrepreneurship education, academic coordination, student development, and knowledge sharing.

Alongside his professional responsibilities, he is pursuing advanced studies at Rashtriya Raksha University in Gandhinagar, Gujarat, India, reflecting his continued commitment to academic growth and lifelong learning.

His academic background includes an MBA in Entrepreneurship from the Catholic University of Milan, Italy, a Master's degree in Global Business from the University of Makeni, and undergraduate qualifications in business administration and accounting from Stella Maris Polytechnic University.

Over the course of his professional journey, Titus has developed experience in audit, accounting, taxation, regulatory compliance, risk management, business development, and entrepreneurship.

These experiences have shaped his belief that education should extend beyond the classroom. He believes in connecting academic knowledge with practical experience and using education as a tool for individual and institutional development.

His professional philosophy is grounded in lifelong learning, knowledge sharing, mentorship, collaboration, and the development of people and organizations.
