ENTREPRENEURSHIP EDUCATION

Turning Ideas Into Possibilities

Titus Nyuma Korfeh's academic and professional interests are strongly connected to entrepreneurship, business development, innovation, and enterprise creation.

His work in entrepreneurship education focuses on helping students and aspiring professionals understand how ideas can be evaluated, developed, and transformed into practical opportunities.

Entrepreneurship, in this context, extends beyond starting a business. It involves identifying opportunities, solving problems, creating value, managing resources, and developing sustainable solutions.
